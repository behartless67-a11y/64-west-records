SUNDAY REVIVAL

Folk Rock / Americana Duo

Almost Here (2026)

10 tracks celebrating friendship, Sunday traditions, and new beginnings.

A male/female duo blending Fleetwood Mac harmonies with Ron Pope intimacy and Grateful Dead storytelling. Sunday Revival captures the warmth of Sunday afternoons with friends, wine-soaked laughter, patient hope, and the joy of welcoming something precious into the world.

Folk rock for Sunday Fundays, front porch hangs, and life's beautiful transitions.
