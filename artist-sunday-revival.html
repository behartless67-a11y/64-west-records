About Sunday Revival

Sunday Revival is a special one-time collaboration celebrating friendship, Sunday Fundays, and new beginnings. Created as a gift for Sarah and Daniel as they prepare to welcome their baby boy, this album captures years of memories, laughter, and the joy of time spent together.

The music blends folk rock with Americana soul, featuring warm harmonies and heartfelt storytelling. From lazy Sunday afternoons to red wine conversations, from coffee shop mornings to sunset celebrations, these songs honor the simple, beautiful moments that make life rich.

"Almost Here" is about more than music - it's about the anticipation of new life, the gratitude for old friends, and the magic of those perfect days when time seems to slow down and everything feels exactly right.

For Sarah, for Daniel, for their son, and for every Sunday that brought us closer together.

Almost Here (2026)

Folk Rock / Americana • 10 Tracks

"Almost Here" is a celebration album - warm, intimate, and overflowing with joy. The sound is folk rock with Americana soul, featuring acoustic guitars, gentle percussion, and harmonies that feel like gathering around a campfire with your closest friends.

The album opens with "Sunday Afternoon," capturing the lazy, golden feeling of time well spent. "Almost Here" is the title track, written specifically for Sarah and Daniel as they prepare to meet their son. It's about anticipation, love, and the beautiful threshold between what was and what's coming.

"Red Wine & Good Times" and "Coffee Shop Mornings" celebrate the simple rituals of friendship - the conversations that last for hours, the inside jokes, the comfortable silences. "You & Me & The Sunset" is pure gratitude, while "New Beginnings" looks forward with hope and excitement.

"Daniel's Song" and "Sarah's Grace" are personal tributes - honoring the strength, kindness, and love they've shown as friends and soon-to-be parents. "Forever Friends" promises what we already know - that some bonds don't fade with time or distance. And "One More Glass" closes the album with a toast to all of it: the memories made, the moments ahead, and the people who make life worth celebrating.

This is Sunday Revival's only album, and that's exactly as it should be. Some stories are meant to be told once, perfectly.
