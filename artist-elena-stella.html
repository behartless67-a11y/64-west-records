About Elena Voss & Stella Hartley

Elena Voss and Stella Hartley are the two women behind Vexra. Without the LED masks, they make music that is intimate, textured, and deeply personal. Four albums deep across folk-pop, flamenco-influenced acoustic, and hyperpop, their sound shifts with each record while their voices stay unmistakable. Elena smooth and warm, Stella bright and raspy. Together they make something neither could make alone.
