About DJ Snuggle Bear

DJ Snuggle Bear is a beloved children's entertainer, friend to boys and girls everywhere, and a very, very good bear. He has been making sing-along songs for as long as anyone can remember, and possibly for longer than that.

His music features ukuleles, glockenspiels, kazoos, hand claps, and a chorus of cheerful little voices. Every song is designed to be easy to remember and almost impossible to forget. Snuggle Bear sings about counting birdies, brushing your teeth, the friendly Mr. Sun, and the importance of locking your windows at night.

Critics have called Snuggle Bear's music "deeply memorable," "stuck in my head for three days," and "please make it stop." Children love him. Parents are confused by him. He doesn't care either way, because Snuggle Bear is here for the boys and girls.

Snuggle Bear is always watching, always smiling, and always happy to make a new friend. Are you a new friend?

Snuggle Bear Sings for Chris! (2026)

Children's Music / Sing-Along • 10 Tracks • Dedicated to Chris

DJ Snuggle Bear's debut album is a sing-along celebration made just for one very special boy: Chris. Across ten ridiculously catchy tracks, Snuggle Bear teaches Chris about counting, hygiene, healthy snacks, friendly animals, and the importance of locking your windows.

The music is pure preschool sunshine: bouncy ukuleles, sparkly glockenspiels, hand claps, kazoos, and a cheerful chorus of little voices. Every song is engineered to be impossible to forget, with hooks that will live rent-free in Chris's head until the end of time.

Highlights include the joyful greeting song "Hi Hi Hi!", the heartwarming personalized "The Chris Song," the bedtime favorite "My Friend in the Wall," and the tender closing lullaby "Goodnight Christopher" where Snuggle Bear reminds Chris that he is always there. Always.

This is the perfect album for car rides, bath time, and lying awake at three in the morning wondering if you locked the windows. A gift of love. A gift you can never give back.
