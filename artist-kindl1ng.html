KINDL1NG

Collaborative Folk-Electronic • A 64 West Records Project

About

KINDL1NG is the collaborative folk-electronic project of 64 West Records. Each album gathers the label's roster around a unifying theme, trading verses across genres and pairing established artists with new debuts. Folk and electronic, intimate and atmospheric, songs that belong on a porch and songs that belong in a warehouse.

The —light series began with Porchlight, expanded with Hearthlight, and now steps out into the city with Streetlight. Three albums, one growing collective, the same light shifting through different windows.

KINDL1NG features rotating lineups including Vexra, Elena Voss, Sunday Supply, Tyler Hayes & Jake Morrison, Jessie Mae Thornton, HARLOW, Cassidy James, and new male voices Atlas Reign, August Pine, and Cole Hartwell making their label debut on Streetlight.

Sweet Loud Summer

Melodic Bass / Future Bass / Folk-Electronic • 2026 • 16 Tracks

SWEET LOUD SUMMER is the extended fifth KINDL1NG album, a 16-track summer collaboration that flows from peak festival mainstage energy in June and July down into lake-cabin intimacy by late August. KINDL1NG hosts every track and brings the melodic-bass DNA, while a rotating cast of featured artists colors each track with their own genre.

Every existing 64 West Records artist features once (Vexra ×2 exception), and four new talents are introduced: Cameron Lake (male EDM-pop tenor), Marigold (female soul-pop powerhouse), Fern Holloway (female bluegrass clarion), and Olive June (female folk-pop indie). From the first day off in June to the last drive home in August, this is summer in both halves — loud and sweet, mainstage and bonfire, festival wristband and dock light.

Starlight

Melodic Bass / Future Bass / Folk-Electronic • 2026 • 12 Tracks

STARLIGHT is the fourth KINDL1NG album, and the first to look up. After Porchlight, Hearthlight, and Streetlight gathered around increasingly outward sources of warm light, Starlight finally turns toward the night sky and the festival stage beneath it. This is KINDL1NG's Illenium album: melodic bass, future bass, and melodic dubstep production with acoustic guitar threading through verses as texture, big emotional drops, supersaw chord stabs, sidechained pads pumping, soaring vocal anthems over crushing low end.

Twelve tracks, mostly solo to keep voices clean, featuring Vexra, Elena Voss, HARLOW, Atlas Reign, August Pine, Cassidy James, and three new debuts: Sebastian Vale, Wren Halloway, and Nyx Cordell. From the rooftop-stargazing of "Constellation" to the void-walking of "Voidwalker" to the supernova-detonation of "Supernova" to the closer "Daystar" where Vexra and Elena finally share the same song, this is the album where the —light series reaches the stars.

Streetlight

Collaborative Folk-Electronic • Urban Indie • 2026 • 12 Tracks

STREETLIGHT is the third KINDL1NG album, and the first to leave the porch. Where Porchlight and Hearthlight gathered around warm interior light, Streetlight follows the night out into the city. Twelve collaborative tracks across the 64 West roster, joined by three new male voices making their label debut: Atlas Reign, August Pine, and Cole Hartwell.

From Vexra and Atlas Reign trading verses on a rain-slicked crosswalk in "Wet Pavement" to Tyler Hayes & Jake Morrison harmonizing through forty miles of empty highway on "Dashboard Glow," from Elena Voss and August Pine listening to each other through apartment plaster in "Across the Hall" to Sunday Supply walking home into the sunrise on "Walking Home," every track is a different angle on the same warm yellow light. Folk-electronic at its core, but the lamp is the city now.

Hearthlight

Collaborative Folk-Electronic • 2026 • 12 Tracks

The second KINDL1NG album, gathered around the fire. Hearthlight turns inward where Porchlight looked outward, finding the songs that belong by the wood stove on a winter evening. Lush harmonies, warm instrumentation, and the particular intimacy of music made for company.

Collaborative Folk-Electronic • 2026 • 12 Tracks

The KINDL1NG project's debut. Porchlight is the welcome album, the front-step gathering, the songs that travel across the yard on a summer evening. The first time the 64 West roster came together as a collective, trading verses on a porch instead of trading singles in the studio.
