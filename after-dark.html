About This Compilation

12 tracks celebrating summer nights - the chill side of the season when the sun goes down.

From late-night beach walks to back porch hangs, rooftop views to campfire sing-alongs, each artist brings their evening energy. This is summer after dark: peaceful but alive, chill but upbeat, enjoying life with good people under the stars.
