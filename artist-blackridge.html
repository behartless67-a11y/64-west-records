About BLACKRIDGE

BLACKRIDGE is a four-piece heavy rock band from the coal country of Southwest Virginia. Brothers Jake and Luke Hensley (vocals/guitar and drums), childhood friend Marcus Reid (bass), and lead guitarist Eli Cross grew up in Blackridge Hollow, a forgotten coal mining town where the only two options were the mines or music. They chose music.

Their sound is blue-collar metal with Appalachian roots. Think Godsmack meets Disturbed meets Chevelle with Southern grit. Heavy drop-tuned guitars, aggressive drums, raw vocals, and lyrics about survival, defiance, and refusing to break.

"Unbreakable" (2026) is their debut album - 12 tracks of authentic rage, resilience, and triumph. From displacement and anger through finding strength to ultimate victory, it's the story of every kid who didn't fit in, fought back, and made it out. Bonus track "Iron Gospel" is included as their original debut single.

Unbreakable (2026)

BLACKRIDGE's debut album is 12 tracks of raw, authentic Southern hard rock. The arc follows a journey from displacement and anger through finding strength to ultimate triumph. It's the story of every kid who didn't fit in, who was told they'd never make it, who got knocked down and came back swinging.

"Ground Zero" opens with the collapse - divorce, upheaval, everything falling apart. "Wrong Skin" and "Swallow It Down" capture the rage of being the outsider in a hostile small town. "Rust Belt Son" is a working-class anthem about the system stacked against you.

"Bloodline" marks the turning point - reconnecting with real friends who pull you back into the light. "The Hit" is cathartic revenge, that moment you finally stand your ground. "No Apologies" and "Southern Steel" are pure defiance - done being quiet, done bending, proud of your roots but rejecting the hate.

"Higher Ground," "Built Not Given," and "Far From You" are triumph tracks - made it out, built something real, moved on from the doubters. The title track "Unbreakable" closes the album: you bent me but I didn't break, now I'm steel. Bonus track "Iron Gospel" is their original debut single.
