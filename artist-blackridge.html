About BLACKRIDGE

BLACKRIDGE is a four-piece heavy rock band from the coal country of Southwest Virginia. Brothers Jake and Luke Hensley (vocals/guitar and drums), childhood friend Marcus Reid (bass), and lead guitarist Eli Cross grew up in Blackridge Hollow, a forgotten coal mining town where the only two options were the mines or music. They chose music.

Their sound is blue-collar metal with Appalachian roots. Think Black Stone Cherry meets Alice in Chains meets the ghost of every shut-down coal mine in Virginia. Heavy riffs, growling vocals, and lyrics about hard work, hard times, and refusing to back down.

"Iron Gospel" is their debut single and mission statement. It's anthemic, defiant, and unapologetically heavy. BLACKRIDGE isn't trying to be polished or radio-friendly. They're trying to tell the truth about life in the hollers, and sometimes the truth sounds like a freight train running through a thunderstorm.

About This Album

Heavy rock from the hollers of Southwest Virginia. BLACKRIDGE delivers blue-collar anthems with crushing riffs and defiant honesty. This is the sound of coal mines, dirt roads, and refusing to back down. Raw, powerful, and unapologetically Appalachian.

"Iron Gospel" is a six-minute epic that builds from a haunting acoustic intro to a full-throttle metal assault. The lyrics tell the story of generations of coal miners who broke their backs building America, only to be forgotten when the mines shut down.

This is just the beginning for BLACKRIDGE. A full album is in the works, but this single is a perfect introduction to their sound. Heavy, honest, and impossible to ignore.
