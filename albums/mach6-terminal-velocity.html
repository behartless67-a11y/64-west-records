About the Artist

MACH-6 is an anonymous rapid-fire lyricist out of the Virginia underground who fuses tongue-twisting double-time rap with bone-crushing bass music, trap, and dubstep production. Nobody knows who he is. He performs behind a blacked-out motorcycle visor. His flow is impossibly fast, his beats hit like a freight train. His lyrics are dense with internal rhyme schemes and technical wordplay. He's the rap equivalent of a speed demon, hence the name.
