About the Artists

Tyler Hayes and Jake Morrison are best friends from Charlottesville, Virginia, both 42, married with kids. What started as acoustic guitars at a brewery after a rained-out softball game turned into an accidental music career. They write songs about the stuff nobody else will admit: that you need permission to go out, that hangovers last three days now, and that the wildest part of your weekend is the 2pm brewery tour. Their sound channels Dan + Shay's tight harmonies with lyrics about real life in your 40s.

About This Album

"Aged to Perfection" is party country for people who can't party like they used to. It's 12 brutally honest, hilarious songs about being in your 40s: needing three business days to recover from one night out, getting hall passes from your wife, throwing your back out dancing to the Cupid Shuffle, and peaking at brewery tours instead of nightclubs.

From wine flights replacing shots to Denny's replacing the afterparty, this album captures the evolution from wild twenties to civilized forties with humor, heart, and zero apologies. It's about wineries over bars, brunch over late nights, and sitting down at concerts without shame.

Modern country production with 808s and tight harmonies, but lyrics that speak to everyone who's ever needed their wife's permission to go out or called in sick the day after one beer. Dan + Shay meets real life. Funny, self-aware, and surprisingly sweet.
