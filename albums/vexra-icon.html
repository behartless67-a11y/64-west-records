About the Artist

VEXRA emerged from the underground bass scene in Virginia, an anonymous producer and vocalist who refused to show her face. Performing in a custom LED mask and all black, she built a cult following on the strength of her music alone. No face. No name. Just VEXRA. Her production sits at the collision point of emotional pop vocals and skull-rattling trap, bass house, and hardstyle. Think Krewella's raw vulnerability fused with Yellow Claw's aggression, filtered through a cyberpunk lens. Over five albums, VEXRA evolved from anonymous masked performer to revealing her true identity as Elena Voss, integrating both sides of herself, and finally emerging as one complete woman who chose to keep the name that made her powerful.

About This Album

"Icon" is VEXRA's mainstream takeover, on her own terms. After five albums of evolution from anonymous basement producer to integrated artist, Elena Voss knows exactly who she is and what she wants: everything. This album tackles the tension between underground credibility and mainstream ambition with meta-commentary, self-awareness, and zero apologies.

The sound is hyperpop chaos meets dark pop accessibility. Think 100 gecs' glitchy maximalism + SOPHIE's PC Music innovation + Billie Eilish's whispery menace + Charli XCX's pop brilliance. Pitched vocals, distorted bass, metallic synths, explosive choruses, and hooks designed to dominate both TikTok and festival stages.

Lyrically, VEXRA addresses the accusations head-on: "Sellout," "Industry Plant," "Radio Edit." She's not defensive. She's declarative. "I want the money, I want the fame, and I'm not compromising my art to get it." The album is a feminist statement about ambition: women are punished for wanting success in ways men never are. VEXRA refuses to play small.

"Mainstream Damage" serves as a full-circle moment, referencing her debut "Beautiful Damage" while acknowledging the journey from basement to Billboard. "TikTok" satirizes the algorithm-driven music industry while delivering the exact kind of hook that could go viral. "Festival Headliner" and "Billboard" manifest her goals in real-time.

The closing track "Billboard" is the victory lap. Not celebrating what she's achieved, but declaring what's already hers, just waiting to be claimed. This is VEXRA at her most confident, most ambitious, and most unapologetically herself. The underground princess is coming for the crown, and she's bringing all her darkness with her.
