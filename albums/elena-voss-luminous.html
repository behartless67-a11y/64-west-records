About the Artist

Elena Voss is the woman behind the mask. For years, she was known only as VEXRA, an anonymous producer and vocalist in the underground bass scene. Through six albums, she evolved from masked performer to revealing her identity, integrating both sides of herself. "Luminous" is her first solo album under her own name, stepping away from VEXRA entirely to make art on her own terms. No bass drops, no masks, no proving anything. About This Album

Elena's first solo album, completely separate from VEXRA. After "Icon" proved she could dominate the mainstream, Elena steps away to make art on her own terms. No masks, no bass drops, no proving anything. Just her voice, beautiful production, and emotional depth.

The sound is ethereal electronic and art pop. Think FKA twigs meets James Blake meets Imogen Heap. Layered harmonies, glitchy textures with warmth underneath, experimental but accessible. This is Elena showing the world she can make you cry with just her voice and a melody.

The album explores the peace that comes after integration. It's about being present in your own life, mature love that isn't dramatic but real, finding beauty in stillness, and confidence that doesn't need volume. The opening tracks establish the new sonic territory and emotional headspace. Middle tracks explore relationships, self-love, and presence. Closing tracks achieve transcendence without needing to be loud about it.

"Luminous" is a different kind of power. Not the aggression of VEXRA, but the strength that comes from being whole. Elena proves that softness isn't weakness, and quiet can be just as impactful as chaos. This is her artistic statement, separate from VEXRA entirely, and it's beautiful.
