About Winter Sage

From the Shenandoah Valley, Virginia. Winter Sage spent her 20s living in a mountain cabin writing brutally honest songs about mental health, complicated relationships, and the struggle to belong.

Early 30s, queer, writes about relationships and experiences that don't fit neat boxes. The kind of artist people discover at 2 AM when they're falling apart.

Makes devastatingly honest music - no filter, no pretense, just raw truth about healing and finding home in yourself. Her powerhouse vocals can crack open the most intimate whisper or build to earth-shaking climaxes.

This is music for anyone who's ever felt frozen by depression, trapped by a toxic relationship, or lost in the darkness wondering if spring will ever come. Winter Sage has been there, and she writes the songs you need to hear.

Thaw (2026)

Indie Folk / Americana / Chamber Folk • 12 Tracks

Emotional winter to spring metaphor. An album about coming out of depression, isolation, and a toxic relationship. Each song represents the slow thaw from frozen isolation to blooming again.

From the frozen numbness of "Frostbite" through the claustrophobic struggle of "Cabin Fever" to the terrifying first crack of "Ice Break," the album tracks the slow, painful, beautiful process of coming back to life.

Raw vulnerability meets powerhouse vocals - intimate indie folk that builds to devastating climaxes. Winter's voice can hold you close in a whisper or shake you to your core with its power.

By the time "Home in My Bones" closes the album, you've journeyed from frozen isolation to full bloom, from barely surviving to thriving. This is healing made music, transformation rendered in song.
