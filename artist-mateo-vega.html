Mateo Vega

Heartbreak in Spanish

About

Mateo Vega is 64 West Records' Latin pop heartbreak specialist, bringing a fresh bilingual perspective to the universal story of emotional unavailability. With smooth vocals and infectious reggaeton-infused production, Mateo explores the complicated territory of modern romance where connection and commitment constantly clash.

His debut album "Corazón Frío" (Cold Heart) tells the story of someone who craves intimacy but sabotages every relationship before it can become real. Through bilingual Spanish/English lyrics and modern Latin production featuring dembow rhythms and trap influences, Mateo captures the paradox of wanting love while keeping everyone at arm's length.

Drawing from reggaeton, Latin trap, and contemporary pop production, Mateo Vega creates radio-ready tracks that balance club-ready energy with raw emotional vulnerability. His music speaks to anyone who's ever been afraid to let someone in — or fallen for someone who was.

Corazón Frío (2026)

Latin Pop / Reggaeton • 12 Tracks

Corazón Frío is Mateo Vega's debut album chronicling emotional unavailability and the fear of intimacy wrapped in infectious Latin pop production. Through 12 tracks of bilingual Spanish/English storytelling, Mateo explores the paradox of wanting connection while being terrified of vulnerability — the cycle of attraction, self-sabotage, and loneliness that defines modern commitment-phobia.

Blending reggaeton, Latin trap, and modern pop production with dembow rhythms, trap-influenced percussion, and smooth melodic vocals, Corazón Frío creates club-ready energy with raw emotional depth. The production draws from contemporary Latin urban music while maintaining radio-friendly accessibility, featuring infectious hooks, atmospheric synth work, and rhythmic foundations that make heartbreak danceable.

The album progresses through the complete cycle of emotional unavailability: the rush of new attraction balanced with instant panic about commitment, the pattern of keeping things casual to avoid getting hurt, the self-awareness that you're the problem but inability to change, and finally the lonely aftermath of pushing everyone away. Standout tracks include the contradictory opener "Fuego y Hielo" (Fire and Ice), the club anthem "Noche Loca" (Crazy Night), the vulnerable confession "Vulnerable," and the haunting title track "Corazón Frío" that captures the loneliness of emotional walls.
