About Tyler Hayes & Jake Morrison

Tyler Hayes and Jake Morrison are best friends from Charlottesville, Virginia. Both 42, both married with kids, both stuck in that weird middle-age zone where you still want to go out but your body vetoes the decision three days later.

What started as acoustic guitars at a brewery after yet another rained-out softball game turned into an accidental music career. They write songs about the stuff nobody else will admit: needing your wife's permission to have boys' night, throwing your back out at weddings, and discovering that your peak weekend moment is now a 2pm brewery tour.

Their sound channels Dan + Shay's polished harmonies and modern country production, but with lyrics about real life in your 40s. It's party country, technically. Just… a different kind of party. The kind that starts at brunch and ends by 9pm.

About This Album

"Aged to Perfection" is party country for people who can't party like they used to. It's 12 brutally honest, hilarious songs about being in your 40s: needing three business days to recover from one night out, getting hall passes from your wife, throwing your back out dancing to the Cupid Shuffle, and peaking at brewery tours instead of nightclubs.

From wine flights replacing shots to Denny's replacing the afterparty, this album captures the evolution from wild twenties to civilized forties with humor, heart, and zero apologies. It's about wineries over bars, brunch over late nights, and sitting down at concerts without shame.

Modern country production with 808s and tight harmonies, but lyrics that speak to everyone who's ever needed their wife's permission to go out or called in sick the day after one beer. Dan + Shay meets real life. Funny, self-aware, and surprisingly sweet.

First Warm Weekend (2026)

Americana / Country Rock • 12 Tracks

"First Warm Weekend" celebrates the arrival of Virginia spring after a brutal winter. This is Tyler and Jake at their most authentic - celebrating the first jacket-free day, walking to the brewery down the street, hitting Durty Nellys (their beloved grungy dive bar), and turning Saturday day drinking into Sunday Funday without apology.

The album captures Virginia life perfectly: back porch rituals, winery trips (beer drinkers trying to be classy), small town Saturday nights, and that annual "let's wake up early and drive to Virginia Beach" plan that usually fails but lives in legend. From "Back Porch Revival" to "Country Roads & Cold Beer" to the emotional centerpiece "One More Round," these songs celebrate friendship, spring renewal, and the simple joy of cold beer and warm nights.

Modern Americana production with authentic country rock soul. Brotherhood harmonies, honest storytelling, zero pretension. This is spring in Virginia - Blue Ridge mountains, dogwood blooms, dive bars, breweries, and friends who feel like family. Pure celebration of the moments that make life worth living.

Backyard Sessions (2026)

Party Country / Summer Anthems • 12 Tracks

The ultimate summer party country album. Tyler and Jake make feel-good anthems about backyard BBQs, cold beers, cornhole tournaments, and legendary summer nights with your best friends. Think Florida Georgia Line meets Old Dominion - modern country party energy with great harmonies and radio-ready hooks.

From the anthemic opener "Backyard Kings" (claiming the throne of legendary gatherings) to "Cooler Talk" (best conversations happen standing around a cooler full of beer), every track celebrates the simple joy of summer with your crew. "Propane & Possibilities" brings hardware store philosophy, "Cornhole Champions" captures competitive backyard game energy, and the title track "Backyard Sessions" is a full-band celebration of all these summer memories.

This is country music for people who know the best moments in life happen in the backyard - string lights overhead, grill smoking, music playing, and good friends gathered around. Perfect for tailgates, BBQs, and any summer party that's about to become legendary.
