# JESSIE MAE THORNTON - "Dirty Myrtle"
## COMPLETE ALBUM - 14 Tracks

**Artist:** Jessie Mae Thornton
**Genre:** Country Rock / Coastal Country / Reggae-Country
**Label:** 64 West Records
**Year:** 2026
**Runtime:** ~50 minutes

**Concept:**
A road trip to Myrtle Beach — the whole arc. The drive down, the arrival, the reunion with an old friend, the beach days, the BBQ nights, the dancing, the slow mornings, the bittersweet drive home. Pure summer joy — no heartbreak, no loss, no complicated feelings. Just the specific happiness of being exactly where you want to be with exactly the right people.

**The vibe:** Zac Brown meets Chris Stapleton meets early Miranda Lambert. Electric guitar up front, Southern rock muscle, two reggae-country grooves where the beach demands them, one stripped-back ballad where the joy gets quiet and honest. All major keys. All happy. The whole album is a postcard.

**Album title note:** "Dirty Myrtle" is what the locals call Myrtle Beach — a little rowdy, a little sun-soaked, completely lovable. Exactly right.

**Sound palette:**
- Rock country tracks: Electric guitar lead, hard driving drums, chunky rhythm section, pedal steel accents
- Reggae-country tracks: Reggae skank guitar, one-drop drums, walking bass, shakers, pedal steel sighing
- Ballad: Stripped acoustic, fiddle, barely there drums, close mic intimate
- All organic, all live-band feel
