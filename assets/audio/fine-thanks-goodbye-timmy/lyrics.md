# FINE THANKS - "Goodbye Timmy"

**Release Date:** 2026
**Genre:** Loud Indie Rock / Post-Punk / Garage Rock
**Label:** 64 West Records
**Format:** 7-track EP (personal edition for Jason)

**Tracklist:**
1. Timmy
2. Out of Office (Forever)
3. Performance Review (angry)
4. I Am Your Supervisor
5. Let's Take This Offline
6. Gardening Leave
7. World's Best Boss (closer)
**Concept:** A useless, unqualified, perpetually-absent supervisor named Timothy ("Timmy") gets put on a performance review and abruptly rage-quits. Goodbye-Toby-from-The-Office energy: barely-contained glee, savage but gleeful not bitter. Real names used (Timmy, Jason) for the personal gift version.

## Voices (Fine Thanks duet format)
- **Jess (Voice 1):** anxious overthinker, higher/faster, Southern England, nasal, defensive ("we shouldn't celebrate this")
- **Sam (Voice 2):** cynical/dismissive, lower/slower, Manchester, rougher, sardonic bite (buries him every time)

## Running gags
- "I am your supervisor" — Timmy's real catchphrase, weaponized by Sam
- Out on every busy day, but always free to book a 90-minute meeting
- Performance review -> abrupt quit

## Suno note
- Do NOT split/hyphenate the name in lyrics. "Timmy" un-split sings correctly as Tim-MEE. "Tim-my" / hyphenated makes Suno read "my" (rhymes with eye). Keep hook words un-hyphenated.
