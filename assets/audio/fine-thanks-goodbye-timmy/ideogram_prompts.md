## Design Notes

**Key Visual Elements:**
- Two women (Jess and Sam) - distinct, deadpan, secretly delighted (or no people, just the absence)
- Drab open-plan British office: beige cubicles, grey carpet, fluorescent light
- Goodbye-Toby props: supermarket sheet cake, "Sorry You're Leaving" card, drooping balloon, party hats, abandoned lanyard, empty swivel chair
- Muted desaturated palette: greys, beige, cream, sickly fluorescent white
- Vintage film grain, lo-fi documentary intimate style
- British emotional repression / barely-contained relief

**Text Overlay:**
- "FINE THANKS" - thin simple sans-serif, top
- "Goodbye Timmy" - smaller lowercase, bottom
- Understated, integrated, minimal

**Mood:** Deadpan office misery that is secretly the best day of everyone's life. Dark humour through visuals, unglamorous authenticity.

---

## Color Palette Reference

- Slate grey: #6B7280
- Warm cream: #F5F0E8
- Muted beige: #D4C8B8
- Sickly fluorescent white: #F0F2EC
- Cool grey: #9CA3AF

**Avoid:** Bright saturated colours, high contrast, warm vibrant tones, clean corporate glamour.

**Goal:** Desaturated, muted, fluorescent-lit office melancholy - like a grey British Tuesday where the worst person you know just quit.
