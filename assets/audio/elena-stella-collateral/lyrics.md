# Elena Voss & Stella Hartley - "Collateral"
## COMPLETE ALBUM - 10 Tracks

**Artists:** Elena Voss & Stella Hartley
**Genre:** Hyperpop / Dark Pop
**Label:** 64 West Records
**Year:** 2026
**Runtime:** ~34 minutes

**Concept:**
Everything they've broken, complicated, crashed, and left better. The aftermath is the whole album. Not an apology — a receipt. PC Music / Charli XCX "360" energy: sharp, glitchy, explicit, fast. Two real women being chaotic and joyful and not sorry. NOT Vexra — no masks, no darkness, no menace. Just teeth in a smile.

**Spanish rule:** Only where it's natural. "Roto" (Track 5) is the full Spanish track. One or two organic drops elsewhere. Never forced.

**Voice profiles:**
- **Elena Voss:** Smooth warm soprano pushed through hyperpop production — bright, pitched, layered. Still warm underneath the glitch.
- **Stella Hartley:** Raspy, distorted, glitchy — [raspy] on EVERY Stella line. Rasp + hyperpop is the sound.

**Production identity:**
- Glitchy synth stabs, distorted 808s, rapid hi-hats
- F minor as home key, varies for texture
- Vocal chops, pitched vocals, PC Music production aesthetic
- Explicit cursing natural — not edgy, just how they talk
- Bright not dark — the chaos is joyful
