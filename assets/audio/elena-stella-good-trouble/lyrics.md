# Elena Voss & Stella Hartley - "Good Trouble"
## COMPLETE ALBUM - 10 Tracks

**Artists:** Elena Voss & Stella Hartley
**Genre:** Folk-Pop / Indie Electronic
**Label:** 64 West Records
**Year:** 2026
**Runtime:** ~38 minutes

**Concept:**
Sequel to "Plainclothes." That album was inward — knowing each other in private. This one is outward — what they do with that knowing in the world. Stella's restlessness becomes the engine, Elena's groundedness becomes the anchor that makes the adventure possible. They get into things. They take chances. They show up when it costs something. Good trouble.

**Relationship:** Creative partners, chosen family. Not romantic. The depth comes from years of making Vexra together and everything that builds between two people who trust each other with the work.

**Sound evolution from Plainclothes:**
- Bigger drops — festival-ready on 3-4 tracks
- More driving percussion throughout
- **SIGNATURE INSTRUMENT: Marimba** — Elena's Latin roots, bridges folk-pop and electronic, distinctive and warm. Marimba accents in verses, marimba-driven rhythmic patterns in pre-choruses, marimba lines woven through drops.
- Elena's acoustic foundation still present but EDM shimmer becomes full shimmer on choruses
- Stella's raspy voice more prominent — equal lead throughout, not just harmonies
- Still 2-genre discipline, leaning harder into electronic side

**Voice profiles (unchanged from Plainclothes):**
- **Elena Voss:** Smooth, warm, soprano, grounded, folk-pop close mic delivery
- **Stella Hartley:** Bright, raspy, higher-register, raw emotional rock edge — [raspy] on every Stella line
