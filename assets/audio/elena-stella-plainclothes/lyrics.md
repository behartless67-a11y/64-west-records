# Elena Voss & Stella Hartley - "Plainclothes"
## COMPLETE ALBUM - 10 Tracks

**Artists:** Elena Voss & Stella Hartley
**Genre:** Folk-Pop / Indie Electronic
**Label:** 64 West Records
**Year:** 2026
**Runtime:** ~36 minutes

**Concept:**
Elena Voss and Stella Hartley are the two women behind Vexra. When the masks are off it's just them. "Plainclothes" is the album they make when nobody's performing — no LED masks, no festival drops, no personas. Just two distinct voices who know each other completely, making something together that neither could make alone.

Elena grounds things. Stella electrifies them. Neither is wrong. Both are right.

The sound: acoustic foundation (fingerpicked guitar, piano) with electronic shimmer building through pre-choruses into warm melodic drops. Maggie Rogers production sensibility — organic meets electronic, intimate meets expansive. Every track is a genuine duet: 50/50 voice weight, both essential.

**Voice profiles (CRITICAL for Suno):**
- **Elena Voss:** Smooth, warm, soprano, folk-pop close mic delivery, grounded, the one who settles
- **Stella Hartley:** Bright, raspy, higher-register, raw emotional rock edge, restless, the one who pushes

**Vocabulary rules:**
- No Vexra imagery (no masks, no drops, no festival)
- Concrete and intimate — rooms, cups of coffee, windows, hands, silences
- The electronic elements are shimmer and warmth, not power and aggression
- Each track is about a specific facet of knowing someone deeply

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**The album arc:**
- Tracks 1-4: Establishing the dynamic and the concept
- Tracks 5-6: Each voice gets their own track (The Loud One / Anchor Point) — the album's emotional core
- Tracks 7-8: The harder, more honest material (B minor / different paths)
- Tracks 9-10: Resolution and return — being known, then silence

**The one B minor track (Track 7):**
"Honest Hours" is the only minor key track. Not because it's sad — because it's serious. The honest 2AM conversations deserve a different tonal gravity than everything else.

**The Reprise closer:**
Track 10 strips everything. No electronic shimmer, no drums, barely piano. Just two voices and one guitar. The callbacks to earlier tracks ("You never finish your sentences," "Same ceiling," "Different doors") make it land harder if you've listened all the way through.

**Voice consistency:**
Every single Stella line has [raspy] to ensure Suno maintains her voice. Elena's warmth is in the production description and her bracket tags. Neither voice should ever blend into the other.

**2-genre discipline:**
All prompts use exactly 2 genres (folk-pop + indie electronic / ambient electronic / acoustic). BPM and key are modifiers. All prompts 570-720 chars.
