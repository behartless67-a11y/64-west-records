# FINE THANKS - "Cheers, We Guess"
## COMPLETE ALBUM - 11 Tracks

**Artists:** Fine Thanks
**Genre:** British Indie Rock / Post-Punk
**Label:** 64 West Records
**Year:** 2026
**Runtime:** ~38 minutes

**Duo Members:**
- **Sam** (Voice 1) - FEMALE, lower/cynical Northern England (Manchester), deadpan, slow, devastating
- **Jess** (Voice 2) - FEMALE, higher/anxious Southern England, fast, spiraling, scandalized

**Album Concept:**
Two British women observing the American Fourth of July from the inside. Not trash talk — genuine bewildered affection. The comedy is in the SCALE of everything. The flags. The fireworks starting Thursday. The heat. The dogs. The word "freedom" appearing in every single sentence. Sam delivers observations flat like a nature documentary narrator. Jess cannot process any of it. Underneath: they're absolutely charmed and won't fully admit it until track 11.

**The tone rule:** Punching UP at the spectacle, never down at the people. America is wonderful and completely unhinged and Fine Thanks loves it from a safe distance of about three feet.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**The tone throughout:**
Never punching down. Always punching at the spectacle with bewildered affection. Sam and Jess are not superior — they're overwhelmed and charmed and won't admit it until Track 11. Track 11 is where the whole album lands.

**The arc:**
- Tracks 1-5: Observation and cataloguing (flags, fireworks, merch, parking)
- Track 6-7: The specific tender bits (the dogs, the 2AM person)
- Track 8-9: Genuine emotional moments hiding in comedy (Freedom speech, grandma with biscuits)
- Track 10: Physical surrender to the heat
- Track 11: Full admission that they love all of it

**The through-lines:**
- The eagle from Track 5 returns in Track 11 (it's on the mantelpiece)
- Gerald the dog gets a proper goodbye in Track 11
- The digestives grandma gets a mention
- The 2AM firework person is appreciated
- The Twenty Dollar Parking Man is accepted

**The comedy rule:**
The funniest line is always the flattest. Sam delivering "The dog's name is Freedom. That's Gerald's neighbour. Freedom." straight is funnier than any amount of escalation.

**Genre:** 2 genres each (indie rock + garage punk / post-punk / jangle pop). All prompts 610-650 chars, well under 900.
