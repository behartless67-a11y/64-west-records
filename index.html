About 64 West Records

A fictional indie label from Virginia with artists spanning country, EDM, rap, island reggae, and hard rock. Every artist is connected by the recurring "porch light" motif: the idea that there's always a light on, always a place to come home to.

The label roster is deliberately diverse in genre but unified in authenticity and Virginia roots.
