About Cassidy James

Cassidy James is the voice of your wildest hotel weekend, the friend who convinces you to extend checkout just one more hour, the pop star who makes innuendo an art form without ever crossing the line into explicit territory.

Her debut album "Late Checkout" is a masterclass in suggestive pop—12 tracks of hotel and travel metaphors that let your mind fill in all the delicious blanks. She channels the playful confidence of contemporary pop maximalism with a 70s disco funk revival aesthetic: vintage Moog synthesizers, funky slap bass, live string arrangements, and punchy horn sections create a warm retro-modern soundscape.

Vocally, Cassidy shifts effortlessly from breathy, intimate verses to explosive, powerfully belted choruses. It's the contrast that makes her songs impossible to ignore—she'll whisper a confession in your ear then belt the hook so hard you'll want to scream along.

Her lyrics weaponize everyday experiences: room service becomes foreplay, minibar tabs become measures of commitment, do not disturb signs become declarations of intent. She's self-aware, funny, flirtatious, and never takes herself too seriously—but the vocals and production are serious as hell.

Cassidy James is the soundtrack to your next late checkout. Whether you actually book that hotel room or just daydream about it is entirely up to you.

Late Checkout (2026)

Pop / Disco Funk Pop / 70s Revival • 12 Tracks

The debut album that turns temporary hotel stays into permanent pop anthems. "Late Checkout" weaponizes travel and hospitality metaphors into 12 tracks of suggestive brilliance—never explicit, always clever, letting your imagination do the heavy lifting.

From the flirty opener "Room Service" to the confident empowerment anthem "Frequent Flyer," Cassidy James takes you on a journey through late-night flights ("Red Eye"), accidental extended stays ("Layover"), morning-after reflections ("Continental Breakfast"), and emotional departures ("Checkout").

The production channels 70s disco funk with modern pop polish: warm vintage synthesizers, groovy bass lines, live string arrangements, punchy horn sections, and infectious mid-tempo rhythms. Every track features Cassidy's signature vocal dynamic—breathy intimate verses that explode into powerfully belted choruses.

This is pop music for grown folks who appreciate wordplay, innuendo, and the art of suggestion. It's funny, confident, self-aware, and impossibly catchy. Whether you're actually booking that late checkout or just living vicariously through your headphones, this album is your first-class ticket to somewhere much more interesting than home.
