About This Compilation

12 tracks celebrating resilience, survival, and defiant triumph.

We've been through hell. We've been knocked down, beaten up, and counted out. But we're still here. This compilation captures that "Here's to Us" energy - acknowledging the struggle while celebrating the fact that we made it through.

From country rock anthems to heavy industrial power, from EDM celebration to emotional gratitude, these songs span genres but share one truth: we're survivors. Tyler Hayes & Jake Morrison bring brotherhood energy, Blackridge delivers aggressive defiance, VEXRA brings electronic triumph, Jessie Mae Thornton offers wisdom and release, and Colt Ramsey celebrates working-class resilience.

This is for everyone who's still standing. Raise your glass. You earned it.
