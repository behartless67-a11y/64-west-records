Lila Monroe

Dark Alt-Pop / Moody EDM-Pop

About

Lila Monroe is 64 West Records' breakout voice chronicling the complete journey from toxic love to triumphant self-discovery. Across two albums, she charts an emotional transformation: Undertow (2026) captured the suffocating pull of unhealthy relationships through dark alt-pop, while Afterglow (2026) celebrates the radiant healing that follows with euphoric EDM-pop.

Her sound evolved from moody trap-pop to Zedd-influenced uplifting electronic production, but her signature breathy intimate vocals and emotionally raw storytelling remain constant. Drawing influence from Selena Gomez's confessional vulnerability and Zedd's euphoric production, Lila creates accessible pop that balances radio-ready polish with deep emotional resonance.

Where Undertow pulled you under, Afterglow lifts you up — a healing album wrapped in bright synthesizers, four-on-floor kicks, and triumphant drops. Together, these albums tell the complete story: falling into darkness, and emerging into light.

Undertow (2026)

Dark Alt-Pop • 12 Tracks

Lila Monroe's debut album Undertow is a raw, unflinching chronicle of the complete toxic relationship cycle. From the dangerous magnetic pull of falling hard against your better judgment, through the identity loss and explosive conflicts, to the breaking point and finally choosing yourself, this 12-track journey captures the emotional undertow that drags you under in unhealthy love.

Blending moody ballads with uptempo dance-pop bangers, Undertow showcases Lila's breathy intimate vocals over trap-pop production, atmospheric synth pads, and piano foundations with 808 bass. The album's sound draws from Selena Gomez's confessional vulnerability, Zedd-era EDM-pop energy, and Billie Eilish's minimalist darkness, creating accessible radio-ready tracks that never sacrifice emotional depth.

The album progresses through four acts: The Fall (tracks 1-4) captures dangerous attraction and ignoring warnings. The Deep End (tracks 5-8) explores identity loss and unhealthy attachment. Breaking Point (tracks 9-10) marks realization and liberation. The Aftermath (tracks 11-12) processes healing and choosing yourself. Standout tracks include the moody opener "Undertow," the EDM-pop anthem "See Me," the raw ballad "Breaking Point," and the triumphant closer "Undertow (Reprise)."

Afterglow (2026)

Uplifting EDM-Pop • 12 Tracks

Lila Monroe's triumphant second album Afterglow chronicles her transformation from darkness to light after escaping toxic love. Where Undertow captured suffocating relationships through dark alt-pop, Afterglow celebrates healing, rediscovery, and radiance through euphoric EDM-pop production inspired by Zedd's ability to marry emotional vulnerability with dance-ready energy.

The album follows a deliberate emotional arc across three acts: The Relief (tracks 1-4) explores the immediate aftermath of leaving — breathing freely, recognizing patterns, learning self-worth. The Rediscovery (tracks 5-8) documents rebuilding — reconnecting with forgotten dreams, embracing self-love, reclaiming your voice. The Light (tracks 9-12) marks full emergence — dancing through healing, finding clarity, setting standards for real love.

Blending breathy intimate vocals with bright synthesizers, four-on-floor kicks, and massive uplifting drops, Afterglow showcases Lila's evolution into euphoric electronic pop. Standout tracks include the radiant opener "Afterglow," the self-love anthem "Golden," the Zedd-homage "Clarity," and the triumphant closer "Afterglow (Reprise)" that brings the journey full circle. This is a healing album wrapped in festival-ready production — proof that emerging from darkness into light deserves to be celebrated.
