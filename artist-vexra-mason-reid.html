Vexra x Mason Reid

EDM Country Fusion / Neon Honky-Tonk

About This Collaboration

When Vexra's EDM energy collides with Mason Reid's country soul, something electric happens. "Neon Honky-Tonk" is the sound of two worlds refusing to stay separate, where bass drops meet banjo riffs, where honky-tonk piano stabs ride over trap hi-hats, and where festival main stages merge with dive bar energy.

This isn't a compromise between genres, it's a celebration of both. Vexra brings her cyberpunk intensity and progressive house production, while Mason Reid delivers authentic country storytelling and that late-90s Toby Keith swagger. Together, they've created a fusion that honors both traditions while blazing a completely new trail.

From the opening "Electric Rodeo" to the epic closer "Sunrise Over Silicon Valley & Shenandoah," this 13-track album proves that country and EDM aren't opposites, they're two sides of the same rebellious spirit.

About This Album

"Neon Honky-Tonk" is a genre-defying journey through 13 tracks that prove EDM and country aren't just compatible, they're two expressions of the same rebellious energy. The album opens with the bass house fury of "Electric Rodeo" and builds through progressive anthems, emotional ballads, and aggressive bangers before closing with the epic 8-minute "Sunrise Over Silicon Valley & Shenandoah."

This collaboration showcases the full range of what happens when you mix pedal steel guitar with analog synths, when four-on-the-floor kicks meet boot-stomping rhythms, when warehouse raves collide with tailgate parties. Tracks like "Whiskey & Wire" explore the tension between tradition and future, while songs like "Barn Burner" throw caution to the wind and just let the fusion explode.

At its core, "Neon Honky-Tonk" is about identity, for anyone who's ever felt caught between two worlds, two sounds, two versions of themselves. It's proof that you don't have to choose. You can honor your roots while reaching for something new. You can be country and techno, analog and digital, past and future, all at once.
