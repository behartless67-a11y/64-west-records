About Sunday Supply

Sunday Supply is a five-piece Americana/Southern rock band from Crozet, Virginia, built around the 25+ year partnership of Cole Harrison (44) and Lena Brooks (42), high school sweethearts who never stopped making music together.

Cole, a Lexington, VA native, and Lena, a California transplant who moved to the Shenandoah Valley as a kid, met in high school and have been together ever since. After spending their adult lives in their hometown with both families nearby, they made the leap during COVID - packing up and moving to Crozet (just outside Charlottesville) to start fresh in the Blue Ridge foothills.

What started as front-porch jam sessions in their new mountain home turned into something bigger when they met local musicians at a Charlottesville brewery. The full band formed naturally: pedal steel player Jake Morrison, drummer Sarah Chen, and bassist/harmony vocalist Marcus Reid rounded out the sound - rootsy, authentic, and built for both the party and the quiet moment after.

The name "Sunday Supply" comes from their weekly ritual: load up on supplies (beer, groceries, good company), head to the mountains, and remember what actually matters. Lena brings California sunshine energy and soaring harmonies, Cole anchors with Virginia storytelling and weathered vocals. Together they write songs about high school love that lasted, families still back home, trading comfort for adventure, and building a life wherever you land.

Their sound? Zac Brown Band meets Jason Isbell - Southern rock with Americana soul, upbeat anthems with real emotional weight. Music for people who love a cold beer, a mountain sunset, and the person sitting next to them.

About This Album

"Key West to Crozet" is the journey from who you were to who you became - hometown roots, cross-country love, pandemic pivots, and finding home in the mountains. It's songs for tailgates, bonfires, brewery patios, and the drive home when you're feeling grateful.

Recorded live at The Southern in Charlottesville with minimal overdubs to capture the band's organic, jam-friendly energy. Pedal steel and harmonies are front and center, anchoring songs that range from rowdy anthems to front-porch ballads.

From the title track's celebration of Sunday rituals to the emotional weight of "When We Left Home," this album captures what happens when you trade comfort for adventure - and discover that home isn't a place, it's the people you bring with you. Zac Brown Band energy meets Jason Isbell storytelling, with real stories about high school parking lots, Key West sunsets, Virginia homecomings, and the courage it takes to start over.

About This Album

"Coastal" is Sunday Supply's love letter to life on the water. Trading mountain sunsets for ocean horizons, this 12-track journey blends yacht rock smoothness with Southern rock soul and beach country vibes. Think Doobie Brothers meets Jimmy Buffett with that signature Sunday Supply warmth.

From the laid-back opening of "Coastal Drift" to the triumphant finale "Living Coastal," every song captures the freedom and peace of sailing, seaside living, and letting the tides guide you. Male/female harmonies shine throughout, with clean guitars, yacht rock keyboards, and breezy production that feels like a sunset sail.

Whether you're dropping anchor at the marina, walking barefoot on the boardwalk, or watching harbor lights from the deck, "Coastal" is the soundtrack to ocean living. Southern rock storytelling meets sailing vibes - authentic, uplifting, and made for anyone who knows that sometimes the best direction is wherever the current takes you.
