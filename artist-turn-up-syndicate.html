TURN UP SYNDICATE

Crunk Rap Collective

About

Turn Up Syndicate is a three-member crunk rap group bringing aggressive party energy and heavy 808 bass to 64 West Records. The crew consists of 808 Czar (commanding mid-range authority), Pyro (rapid-fire chaotic wildcard), and Quake (deep bass heavy hitter).

Each member brings distinct vocal style and energy. 808 Czar leads with classic crunk authority, Pyro delivers explosive fast-paced chaos, and Quake shakes foundations with grinding bass power. Together they create relentless party anthems with YEAH/WHAT/OKAY adlibs and speaker-rattling production.

Style: Crunk, Party Rap, Bass Music
Members: 808 Czar, Pyro, Quake
Debut: 2026

No Off Switch

The debut album from Turn Up Syndicate delivers 14 tracks of pure crunk party energy. Each member gets solo showcase tracks while three posse cuts feature all three going off together.
