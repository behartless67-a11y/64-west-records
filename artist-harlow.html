About HARLOW

Raw power meets vulnerability. HARLOW burst onto the scene with "Wildfire Season," a debut album that refuses to play safe. With a voice that channels Bishop Briggs' raspy intensity and gospel-influenced soul, she's carved out her own space in dark alternative pop.

Born from fire and transformation, HARLOW's music is about burning down what doesn't serve you and rising from the ashes stronger. Her sound is minimalist but massive—stomping percussion, heavy sub-bass, and atmospheric synths create a sonic landscape where her voice can unleash its full power.

No polish, no pretense—just raw emotion and defiant empowerment. HARLOW doesn't ask permission. She takes what's hers.
