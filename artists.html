Meet the Artists

The voices of 64 West Records

Elena Voss / VEXRA

7 Albums

From masked underground bass producer to vulnerable solo artist. Elena Voss spent four albums hiding behind VEXRA before slowly revealing herself across "Human," "Venom Heart," and "Icon." "Luminous" is her first album entirely separate from the persona. EDM, dark pop, hyperpop, industrial bass, and ethereal electronic.

EDM / Dark Pop / Hyperpop / Art Pop

Jessie Mae Thornton

3 Albums

From Staunton, Virginia. Daughter of a tobacco farmer and church choir director. Got discovered after a Floyd Country Store performance went viral. Three albums trace her journey from wide-eyed debut to heartbroken healing to triumphant party country. "Porch Light On" inspired the label name.

Modern Country / Americana

Colt Ramsey

1 Album

28-year-old country singer from Smith Mountain Lake, Virginia. His debut "Lake Days & Late Nights" is summer on the water captured in audio form. Modern country production with trap-influenced 808s and Morgan Wallen's grit meets Kane Brown's crossover appeal. Lake life, Blue Ridge views, and Virginia pride.

Modern Country / Party Country / Lake Life

Ben James

1 Album

Virginia-born R&B singer with a voice that can break your heart and put it back together in the same breath. Smooth vocals, lush production, and songs about love, longing, vulnerability, and connection. Teddy Swims meets Daniel Caesar. Heartfelt, soulful, and effortlessly emotional.

R&B / Soul

MACH-6

1 Album

An enigma. No one knows their real name, age, or face. What we do know: they rap faster than anyone alive. Machine-gun syllables over bone-crushing bass and aggressive EDM production. Tech N9ne meets Skrillex. Technical rap mastery meets futuristic electronic soundscapes.

EDM Rap / Chopper Rap

BLACKRIDGE

1 Single

Four-piece heavy rock band from coal country in Southwest Virginia. Blue-collar metal with Appalachian roots. Black Stone Cherry meets Alice in Chains. Heavy riffs, growling vocals, and lyrics about hard work, hard times, and refusing to back down. Raw, powerful, and unapologetically Appalachian.

Heavy Rock / Southern Metal
