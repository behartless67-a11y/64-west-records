TURN UP SYNDICATE x VEXRA

Industrial Bass House / Crunk Collaboration

About

When Turn Up Syndicate (808 Czar, Pyro, Quake) met Vexra's dark industrial bass house production, sacred chaos was born. This collaboration merges relentless crunk energy with menacing electronic soundscapes, creating a blasphemous fusion of bass cathedral worship and party rap aggression.

Vexra brings her signature industrial aesthetic: heavy distorted 808s with warehouse reverb, metallic synth stabs, and speaker-rattling sub-bass. Turn Up Syndicate delivers three distinct vocal styles over her production: 808 Czar's commanding authority, Pyro's rapid-fire chaos, and Quake's deep grinding power.

Religious power imagery meets club destruction. Stained glass meets strobe lights. Sacred meets chaos.

Sacred Chaos (2026)

Vexra produces an entire album for Turn Up Syndicate, blending her signature dark industrial bass house production with their relentless crunk energy. Religious power imagery meets club chaos as 808 Czar, Pyro, and Quake deliver aggressive party anthems over menacing industrial soundscapes.

From the opening sermon to the apocalyptic revelation, this is 11 tracks of blasphemous bass worship. Cathedral organs transform into industrial synths. Stained glass shatters under speaker pressure. Holy chaos reigns at 145-160 BPM.

Standout tracks: "Cathedral Bass" (the title anthem), "Blasphemy" (hardest hitting posse cut at 160 BPM), "Baptism by Bass" (Quake's drowning-in-bass showcase), and "Revelation" (epic closer bringing all elements together).
