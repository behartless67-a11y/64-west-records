Your Slutty Sister

Power Pop / Alternative Rock

About

Your Slutty Sister is an alternative rock band from Virginia bringing 90s power pop energy to 2026. With a provocative name and sincere sound, they channel classic Weezer-inspired alt-rock: nasal melodic vocals, distorted power chords, and emotionally honest lyrics about everyday life.

Their self-titled debut features thirteen catchy standalone songs ranging from nostalgic ballads to high-energy anthems, all tied together by authentic vulnerability and garage band spirit. No forced themes, just honest rock songs about whatever matters in the moment.

Your Slutty Sister (2026)

Alternative Rock / Power Pop • 13 Tracks

Self-titled debut delivering pure Weezer-core energy with nasal melodic vocals, crunchy power chords, and catchy hooks. From the introspective "Prescription" to the emotional closer "Whatever Happened to Greg?" and comedy hidden track "My Sister Sarah," these songs don't follow a theme—they just capture authentic experiences with garage band sincerity.

Blue Album meets Pinkerton energy with 90s production values and nerdy vulnerability throughout. Thirteen standalone alternative rock anthems about medication anxiety, awkward crushes, lost friendships, shitty cars, basement shows, and everything in between.
