About Colt Ramsey

Colt Ramsey is a 28-year-old country singer from Smith Mountain Lake, Virginia. He grew up spending summers on the water, winters in deer stands, and weekends playing dive bars around Roanoke. His sound blends Morgan Wallen's stadium-ready hooks with Kane Brown's crossover appeal.

Modern country production with trap-influenced 808s, but still rooted in guitar-driven storytelling. He's the guy who shows up to the lake party with a cooler full of beer, a boat full of friends, and a voice that sounds like sunshine and whiskey.

Colt's music captures the essence of Virginia lake life with references to Blue Ridge views, small-town pride, and the kind of summer nights you never forget. He still lives on Smith Mountain Lake and refuses to leave, saying "the songs come from the water, not Music Row."

About This Album

"Lake Days & Late Nights" is summer on Smith Mountain Lake captured in audio form: golden hour boat rides, bonfires on the shore, dive bar karaoke, truck bed makeout sessions, and 2am Waffle House runs. It's party country with a Virginia twist with references to Blue Ridge views, lake life, and small-town Virginia pride.

The album flows from daytime lake anthems to late-night slow burns, capturing the full arc of a perfect summer weekend. From the stadium-ready title track to the emotional closer "Porch Light On," Colt Ramsey delivers 12 tracks of carefree summer energy and the kind of nights you tell stories about for years.

This is modern country for the boat party generation with heavy 808 bass, trap-influenced production, but still unmistakably country at its core. It's Morgan Wallen's grit meets Kane Brown's crossover appeal, all wrapped in Virginia lake life.
