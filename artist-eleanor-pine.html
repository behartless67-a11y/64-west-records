About

Eleanor Pine creates atmospheric indie folk that feels like watching mist roll over mountains at dawn. Her music is painterly and contemplative, drawing from impressionist art and the quiet moments of Virginia's natural beauty.

With delicate fingerpicking, lush string arrangements, and vocals that whisper rather than proclaim, Eleanor crafts songs that exist in the spaces between sleep and waking. Her debut album "Quiet Hours" captures fleeting moments of peace, nostalgia without sentimentality, and the beauty of simply being present.

Eleanor's music is for morning coffee, rainy afternoons, and anyone who finds solace in stillness.

Quiet Hours

Indie Folk • Atmospheric • 2026 • 10 Tracks

Eleanor Pine's debut album is a collection of gentle, atmospheric folk songs that feel like watercolor paintings come to life. Each track captures a specific moment in time—morning light on water, autumn in Virginia, letters never sent, gardens after rain.

Recorded with minimal production to preserve the intimacy of the performances, "Quiet Hours" features delicate acoustic guitar, subtle string arrangements, and Eleanor's hushed vocals that draw you in like a secret being shared. This is music for reflection, for solitude, for finding beauty in ordinary moments.

Influences include Sufjan Stevens, Iron & Wine, and Nick Drake, but Eleanor's voice is distinctly her own—an artist who understands that sometimes the quietest music speaks the loudest.
