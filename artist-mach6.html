About MACH-6

MACH-6 is an enigma. No one knows their real name, age, or face. What we do know is this: they rap faster than anyone alive. Machine-gun syllables delivered with surgical precision over bone-crushing bass and aggressive EDM production.

Inspired by chopper rap legends like Tech N9ne and Twisted Insane, but with a futuristic, electronic edge. If Skrillex and Busta Rhymes had a kid who grew up in a server farm, that kid would be MACH-6.

"Terminal Velocity" is a technical showcase and an endurance test. Ten tracks of relentless speed, complex rhyme schemes, and production that sounds like a jet engine strapped to a subwoofer. This is rap for people who think most rappers are too slow.

About This Album

Pure speed. MACH-6 raps faster than anyone alive with machine-gun syllables over bone-crushing bass and EDM production. Technical rap mastery meets aggressive electronic soundscapes. Relentless, breathless, unstoppable.

Every track is a showcase of technical ability. Complex internal rhyme schemes delivered at speeds most rappers wouldn't attempt. The production is aggressive, futuristic, and designed to match the intensity of the vocals.

This isn't background music. This is music that demands your attention and refuses to let go. From the opening title track to the self-titled closer, MACH-6 proves that speed and skill aren't mutually exclusive. This is chopper rap for the EDM generation.
