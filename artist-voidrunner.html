About VOIDRUNNER

Born from the blackout. Built for the redline.

VOIDRUNNER is the sound of your heart rate at mile six — when your legs say stop but your brain says fuck that. Fusing the raw aggression of Yellow Claw with the euphoric devastation of ILLENIUM, VOIDRUNNER delivers high-BPM, face-melting EDM built for runners, ravers, and anyone who refuses to slow down.

No face. No name. Just a skull with a speedometer buried past the redline and bass that hits like a freight train at 170 BPM. Anonymous, aggressive, and absolutely relentless.

The debut album "No Brakes" is 12 tracks of pure adrenaline — from the hardstyle chaos of "Pace Yourself (Don't)" at 175 BPM to the triumphant catharsis of "Finish Line." No filler. No cooldowns. No brakes.

About This Album

12 tracks. 150-175 BPM. Zero mercy.

"No Brakes" is a debut album designed as an unbroken adrenaline rush. The first half hits you in the face with aggressive trap drops, hardstyle kicks, and cocky confidence anthems. The back half pivots into emotional melodic bass territory — "Flatline" and "Godspeed" deliver the kind of euphoric devastation that ILLENIUM fans live for — before closing with the triumphant "Finish Line."

Every track was built for movement. Running, lifting, raving, or just needing to feel invincible for 45 minutes. This isn't music that asks you to sit down. This is music that tells you to get up, shut up, and go.

Standout tracks: "God Mode" for pure aggression, "Pace Yourself (Don't)" for the fastest BPM on the label, and "Godspeed" for the emotional gut punch you didn't see coming.
