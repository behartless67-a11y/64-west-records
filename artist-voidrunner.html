About VOIDRUNNER

Born from the blackout. Built for the redline.

VOIDRUNNER is the sound of your heart rate at mile six — when your legs say stop but your brain says fuck that. Fusing the raw aggression of Yellow Claw with the euphoric devastation of ILLENIUM, VOIDRUNNER delivers high-BPM, face-melting EDM built for runners, ravers, and anyone who refuses to slow down.

No face. No name. Just a skull with a speedometer buried past the redline and bass that hits like a freight train at 170 BPM. Anonymous, aggressive, and absolutely relentless.

The album "Overdrive" is 12 tracks of pure adrenaline locked at 170 BPM. Blending hardstyle kicks, trap bass, and synthwave arpeggios, every track is built for the moment you push past your redline. From the cyberpunk chaos of "Neon Sector" to the infinite transcendence of the closer "Infinite," this is music for runners who refuse to quit.

About This Album

12 tracks. 170 BPM. Pure overdrive.

"Overdrive" is VoidRunner 2.0, refined and relentless. Every track locked at exactly 170 BPM, blending hardstyle kicks, trap bass, and synthwave arpeggios into a kinetic wall of sound. Alternating between sci-fi void environments (Neon Sector, Digital Wasteland, Quantum Grid) and pure energy concepts (Velocity Override, Surge, Relentless), each track is a standalone banger designed for maximum running momentum.

Fast rhythmic synth arpeggios open every track at full volume from beat one. No ramp-ups. No slow intros. Just immediate aggressive energy with gritty shouted staccato vocals locked to the rhythm. This is music built for the moment you push past your redline and discover what overdrive really means.

Standout tracks: "Overdrive" (the title track) for maximum redline energy, "Quantum Grid" for sci-fi intensity, and "Infinite" for the epic transcendent closer that never ends.
