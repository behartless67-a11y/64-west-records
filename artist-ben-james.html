About Ben James

Ben James is a Virginia-born R&B singer with a voice that can break your heart and put it back together in the same breath. Growing up listening to everything from Teddy Swims to Daniel Caesar to old-school Marvin Gaye, Ben developed a sound that feels both timeless and completely modern.

He started singing in church choirs before discovering his love for R&B and soul music. His performances are intimate, emotional, and deeply felt. Every note comes from somewhere real.

With "Hey Gurl," his debut album, Ben proves he's one of the most exciting voices in modern R&B. Smooth vocals, lush production, and songs that understand what it means to love, lose, and hope anyway. This is R&B for late nights, long drives, and anyone who's ever felt too much.

About This Album

Smooth R&B meets soul. Ben James delivers heartfelt vocals over lush production, channeling the emotional depth of Teddy Swims with his own unique style. An album about love, longing, vulnerability, and connection.

From the opening track "Always and Forever (Hey Gurl)" to the powerful closer "Unstoppable," Ben takes listeners on an emotional journey through relationships, self-discovery, and the complexity of modern love.

Standout collaborations include "Hellfire & Honeysuckle" featuring Jessie Mae and Vexra, and "Unstoppable" which showcases all three artists at their best. This is R&B that feels real, raw, and deeply human.
