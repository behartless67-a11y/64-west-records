Whiskey Religion (2026)

Outlaw Country / Americana • 12 Tracks

Atlas Reign's debut album. 12 tracks tracing the line between sin and salvation, the bottle and the pew. It opens with "Born Again Sinner" and ends on his knees with "Lord, Forgive Me," and every song between earns the distance.

The record runs through Saturday night confession, the devil dialing collect, mile markers and motel diesel, and a wedding band that means more than the gold it's made of. Outlaw country in the Cody Jinks and Tyler Childers tradition: brushed drums, walking bass, steel guitar that breathes, dobro fills, and a baritone that's been somewhere and isn't sorry it went.

Standouts: "Saturday Night, Sunday Morning" for the thesis statement, "Mile Marker 47" for the slow-burn road ballad, "Holy & Hers" for the love song that doesn't lie, and "Lord, Forgive Me" for the closer that earns its grace.
