About Scarlett Reid

Born and raised in Richmond, Virginia, Scarlett Reid is the daughter of a film composer and a voice actress. She grew up in recording studios watching her father score indie films, absorbing the language of cinematic storytelling through music.

She writes songs that feel like movie scenes - dramatic, visual, emotionally devastating. Her music exists in that rare space where vintage Hollywood glamour collides with raw modern heartbreak, where Adele's vocal power meets Lana Del Rey's dark romance meets sweeping orchestral film scores.

Late 20s, vintage aesthetic, red lipstick always. She makes timeless music that could soundtrack a Hitchcock thriller or a contemporary drama with equal power. Every song is a scene, every album a complete film.

This is music for crying in vintage dresses while drinking wine. This is heartbreak as high art. This is Scarlett Reid.
